Moon Base Mission Trainer

You run a small base on the Moon for 10 days (called sols here). Each day you get a little power. Spend it on Oxygen or Shielding. You cannot save power, so every choice costs something.

Space weather from the Sun can hit the base. Strong shielding protects your crew. Crew health and oxygen must stay above zero.
